1. Who handles your information

The ShopDesk operator provides the application, licensing and connected services. Contact the operator at the privacy address above. Your shop administrator decides which customer, supplier and staff information to enter, who may use the shop and which records to share with support. Customers asking about records held by a shop should contact that shop first; we can help route a request where appropriate.

2. Information used

ShopDesk processes the records you enter: business details, products, stock, purchases, sales, returns, expenses, capital, opening balances, services, gaming sessions, orders, customer and supplier contact details, credit and payment records, notes and reminders. Optional images and receipt details may form part of those records. Avoid entering information that is unnecessary for your shop.

Licensing uses a signed license identifier and conditions, customer or shop name, registered device identifier and label, platform, activation status and timestamps. Cloud access also uses a device proof key, sessions, request identifiers, permissions, shop revisions and security audit information. Signing secrets remain outside customer application packages. The app records payment methods and amounts; it does not ask for banking passwords, card security codes or payment-gateway credentials.

3. Local storage and Cloud Sync

Local storage holds application preferences, appearance and wallpaper, activation state, device keys, cloud recovery caches, pending-save records and any preserved older local business backups. Windows uses operating-system protected storage for sensitive activation material; Android uses native protected storage and its keystore. Protection depends on the device and its security.

Cloud Sync stores the licensed shop’s synchronized business state and recovery revisions on Supabase. Personal notes are separated by device in the cloud. The licensing service runs on Render and uses its licensing database on Supabase. These providers process network requests and may retain operational logs or backups under their service settings. Hosting and network processing may occur outside your location. GitHub hosts customer release files and ShopDesk Academy; visiting it or downloading updates also contacts GitHub.

Authorized devices in the same linked shop synchronize through authenticated, device-bound requests. Master Devices manage regular-device permissions. The licensing owner controls Master promotion and license conditions. Permission checks apply in the backend as well as the interface. Offline authority cannot learn a newer restriction until reconnection; signed offline authorization is time limited. Keep shared devices and operating-system accounts secure.

4. Email-based customer assistance

Contact ShopDesk at vels02132@gmail.com. Your email application or email provider handles the message. We receive the address, issue description and attachments that you choose to send and use them to respond to your request.

ShopDesk v4.0.5 does not include remote support sessions or a support-personnel login that grants access to shop databases. Licensing administration and Master Device permissions remain separate. Do not email passwords, private signing material, license keys or complete business backups. Share only information necessary to explain your issue.

5. Purposes and service access

Information is used to operate your shop workflows, produce bills and reports, synchronize authorized devices, validate licensing, distribute updates, deliver administrator messages, prevent unauthorized access and investigate customer assistance emails. External links open services with their own privacy policies. Only share exported reports and backups with people you trust.

6. Security

ShopDesk uses signed licensing, device-bound cloud proofs, encrypted network transport, server permission checks and database access restrictions. Authentication, device permissions and audit trails reduce unauthorized access. No application, network or backup system can guarantee complete security or recovery. Report a suspected security issue to the privacy contact without sending passwords, signing keys or complete customer databases by email.

7. Retention and shop reset

Synchronized records remain available while the shop uses the service until removed through authorized workflows or an agreed service deletion. Operational and licensing records may be retained for security, contractual needs and legal requirements. Contact us for a specific retention or deletion request; this policy does not promise an unverified automatic retention period.

The v4.0.5 shop-reset workflow requires an online Master, a reviewed inventory and an explicit typed confirmation for the canonical shop. It clears that shop’s current business records, cloud personal-note content and recovery snapshots, advances the shop generation and blocks older-generation uploads and imports. It preserves licensing, device registrations, permissions, invoice sequence and security audit metadata.

Reset lets you review recognized local business copies and explicitly select which copies belong to this shop for deletion. Exact SHA-256 checks protect selected copies that change after review; unselected copies remain preserved. Reset is irreversible in ShopDesk and cannot erase exported files, copies on other devices that have not reconnected, unselected older local backups or copies subject to a provider’s backup retention. The reset screen identifies local-cleanup limitations. Do not assume reset removes licensing records or every external copy. Contact the privacy address if a broader deletion request is needed.

8. Your choices and requests

Shop administrators can manage records, regular-device permissions and permitted exports. You may contact the relevant shop and the ShopDesk privacy address to ask about access, correction, deletion, consent withdrawal where applicable, or a privacy complaint. We may need to verify your identity and authority to protect someone else’s records. Requests are handled subject to the law that applies, legal retention duties and technical limits explained to you.

ShopDesk is intended for business administration. Shop administrators are responsible for lawful collection and use of information, including information about children where relevant. Indian data-protection requirements have phased commencement; this policy does not represent that every statutory provision is already in force or that ShopDesk has a government privacy certification.

9. Policy changes

We may revise this policy when the application, service providers, processing or legal requirements change. The public policy will identify the revised version and material changes. Check this page before sharing sensitive records.
